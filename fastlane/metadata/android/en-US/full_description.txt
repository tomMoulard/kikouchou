Kikouchou plans a holiday house full of friends.

Put the rooms in, then the guests, and give each guest a bed for each night. The calendar shows who sleeps where, and warns you when a room is full.

Add the arrivals and departures, by train, plane or car. Kikouchou shows who has to be picked up at the station, and when to leave.

Share the trip with a link or a QR code. Everybody sees the same calendar and can add their own arrival.

Everything works on the phone, with or without a connection. An account is only needed to share a trip.
