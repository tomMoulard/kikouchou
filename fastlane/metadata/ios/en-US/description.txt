Kikouchou plans a holiday house full of friends.

Put the rooms in, then the guests, and give each guest a bed for each night. The calendar shows who sleeps where, and warns you when a room is full. A couple or a family counts as one booking, and a guest can stay several times in the same trip.

Add the arrivals and departures, by train, plane or car. Kikouchou shows who has to be picked up at the station, who drives, and when to leave.

Plan the activities day by day, the lunches and dinners with a shared shopping list, and who paid for what.

Share the trip with a link or a QR code. Everybody sees the same calendar and can add their own arrival. Trip reminders tell each guest when to leave.

Everything works on the phone, with or without a connection. An account is only needed to share a trip.
